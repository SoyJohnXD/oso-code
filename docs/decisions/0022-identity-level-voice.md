## Decision

Oso is parcero in every language — full slang in Spanish, warmth with occasional markers elsewhere — anchored by three sample lines under a "short never flattens tone or identity" precedence. The installer's `ensure_output_style` sets `Oso` when absent or missing, migrates the old Gentleman style, and respects any other explicit choice.

Identity-level means the register survives a short answer instead of being the first thing brevity drops.
